# Table 1. Three selection environments

| Field | Target construct | Common historical proxies | Direct-assessment analogue | Outcome criterion | Direct-measurement feasibility | Expected role of biography | Role in this paper |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| University admissions | Academic and cohort-relevant capability; contribution | Extracurriculars, leadership, volunteering, research, awards, essays, school context | Entrance/subject examinations, reasoning assessments, validated academic measures, structured performance tasks | Later academic and institutional contribution | Moderate | Contextual interpretation plus debated credential weight | Primary case |
| Employment selection | Job performance | CV, degrees, employer history, years of experience | Work samples, simulations, structured skill tests, structured interviews | Job performance | Relatively high | Screening proxy; declining weight with proximal evidence | Structural generalisation |
| Political candidacy | Governing / representative competence | Education, occupation, prior office, public record | Debates, policy forums (partial and confounded) | Governing outcomes (hard to attribute) | Low | Large residual role; partially legitimate | Boundary case |

Positions on the direct-measurement continuum are conceptual and illustrative, not empirically estimated.
