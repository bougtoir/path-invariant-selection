Table 2. An Evidence-Role Audit of Common Admissions Evidence Sources

| Evidence source | Target attribute typically claimed | Relative proximity | Legitimate roles | Path-invariance audit question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Examination or assessment performance | Subject knowledge, reasoning | Proximal for sampled content; distal for unsampled attributes | Evidential | Which attributes in Z does the task actually sample, and which does it not? |
| Course grades and transcript | Sustained academic performance, self-regulation | Proximal for course-based attainment; varies with grading standards | Evidential; contextual (curriculum offered) | Is credit given for the performance, or for the prestige of the course or school that offered it? |
| Structured interview, multiple mini-interview, or situational judgment test | Interpersonal and professional attributes | Intermediate to proximal, depending on design | Evidential | Does the format sample the attribute, or reward fluency acquired through coaching or familiarity? |
| Portfolio, audition, or practical task | Performance in the domain | Proximal | Evidential; constitutive where practice is part of Z | Is the performance judged, or the setting in which it was developed? |
| Personal statement or essay | Motivation, reflection, writing | Distal to intermediate | Evidential (weak); contextual | What attribute is being inferred, and could it be evidenced more directly? |
| Records of activities, leadership, and awards | Commitment, initiative, contribution | Distal | Evidential; contextual; constitutive if explicitly in Z | Would equivalent evidence of the same attribute from a less visible pathway receive the same weight? |
| Research or work experience | Inquiry or professional skill | Distal unless accompanied by a performance sample | Evidential; constitutive in some professional programs | Is the experience credited, or what the applicant can now do? |
| School and neighborhood context indicators | Opportunity, not an attribute of the applicant | Not evidence of Z | Contextual only | Is context used to interpret D, or as an independent credit or debit? |

Note. Proximity is relative and construct-specific: the same source can be proximal for one attribute and distal for another. Entries are illustrative classifications for audit purposes, not empirical estimates. Z = attributes the institution legitimately targets; D = relatively direct evidence.
