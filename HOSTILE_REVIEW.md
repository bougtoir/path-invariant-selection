## Reviewer A — sociology of higher education / inequality
- *Objection:* The paper risks reading as an argument for test-based selection against holistic review, and against contextual admissions.
- *Resolution:* The text explicitly protects contextual interpretation (inside Z, developmental pathway), never recommends a testing technology, cites the SES–test-score evidence (Sackett et al. 2009), and states the paradox conditionally. The objection section answers "holistic review contextualises disadvantage" directly, acknowledging both mechanisms operate in the same files.
- *Objection:* "Experience–equality paradox" may overclaim a documented pattern.
- *Resolution:* Novelty audit downgraded it to a synthesis label; text says "a conditional mechanism… already visible in the literature".

## Reviewer B — admissions policy
- *Objection:* Operational guidance is thin; offices cannot "directly observe" soft attributes.
- *Resolution:* Diagnostic question added ("what in Z is this evidence of… do we already observe it more directly?"); performance assessment recommended precisely where pathway and competence are entangled; vignette designs make the criterion operationalisable.
- *Objection:* Essay and extracurricular evidence may capture non-cognitive traits tests miss.
- *Resolution:* Covered by the "past behaviour predicts beyond present tests" objection — genuine residual value is exactly what RBV names, not a bug.

## Reviewer C — assessment/validity
- *Objection:* Path invariance restates measurement invariance / incremental validity.
- *Resolution:* Both distinctions stated explicitly with citations (Millsap 2011; Sackett & Lievens 2008): object differs (pathway weight vs. instrument equivalence; normative justification vs. empirical ΔR²).
- *Objection:* "Quality(D) up, RBV down" is only conditionally true.
- *Resolution:* Conditions enumerated in text (coverage, reliability, criterion, omitted attributes, directness); framed as directional expectation, not law.

## Reviewer D — social theory / credentials
- *Objection:* Credentialism and signaling already explain this; the coinages multiply jargon.
- *Resolution:* Two coined terms dropped (proxy capture, credential decay); RBV demoted to descriptive scaffold; theory table (Table 2) specifies the residual question each tradition leaves open.
