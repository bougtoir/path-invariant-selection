# SHE_AUTHOR_GUIDE — Studies in Higher Education requirements (verified 2026-09-26)

Sources: Taylor & Francis "Instructions for authors" page for journal cshe20 (tandfonline.com/action/authorSubmission?show=instructions&journalCode=cshe20); journal overview page (peer-review policy); 2018 editorial (doi 10.1080/03075079.2018.1544196).

## Scope / fit
- International journal; empirical, theoretical and conceptual articles of significant originality.
- Manuscripts must be grounded in higher-education literature and contribute substantive new findings or novel insights.
- Explicitly NOT wanted: think pieces; work unconnected to HE literature; narrow/local topics without international relevance; conceptual pieces without deep theoretical engagement.
- Desk-rejection risk is explicitly acknowledged by the journal; title/abstract/keywords drive referee uptake.

## Length and structure
- Typical manuscript ≤ ~7,000 words **including** tables, references, captions, endnotes. Greatly exceeding invites scrutiny. Include a word count.
- Order: title page (with acknowledgements + funding) → abstract → keywords → main text → acknowledgements → references → appendices → table(s) with captions (individual pages) → figure caption list.
- Abstract: 150 words (required).
- Keywords: 5–6.
- Concise section headings; single quotation marks; quotes ≥40 words indented without quotes.
- No equations or images embedded in the manuscript file.

## Review
- ScholarOne submission; **double-anonymised** refereeing (≥2 referees) → a fully anonymised manuscript file is required (title page as separate document in practice; here we supply an unblinded file with a title page plus a blinded file without author identifiers).
- CrossCheck originality screening.

## References / style
- Taylor & Francis author–date style for SHE = APA (APA-style author–date citations and reference list).
- English; any consistent spelling; non-discriminatory language mandatory; SI units.

## Figures
- Separate files (TIFF, EPS, PostScript preferred; high-resolution PNG acceptable at submission in practice — we supply 600-dpi PNG plus editable PPTX per local convention).
- Do not embed in manuscript; numbered in order of appearance; multi-part labelled Figure 1(a) etc.; captions in a list in the manuscript file; descriptive filenames (Figure1, Figure2a).
- Colour free online; print colour charged → design figures to work in greyscale.

## Other
- No submission or page charges.
- Disclosure statement required; funding acknowledgement format specified on title page.
- Biographical notes not required.
- AI/other editorial policies follow T&F editorial policies (no AI-authorship; disclose substantial AI assistance per T&F policy — noted for local author completion).

## Implications for this manuscript
- Main text target ≈ 4,800–5,400 words (references ~1,200–1,500, tables+captions ~400–600) to stay safely under 7,000 total.
- Keep politics ≤ ~6% (one short section); employment one concise section.
- International framing (not US-only admissions); engage SHE debates (admissions, equity, credentialism, merit).
