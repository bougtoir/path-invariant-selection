# NOVELTY_ASSESSMENT — Path-Invariant Selection

Verdicts: GO / MODIFY / DROP for each coined concept. Decisions based on LITERATURE_MAP.md overlap notes.

## Path-Invariance Principle — GO (with explicit disambiguation)
- Not measurement invariance: Millsap (2011) and the psychometric tradition concern whether the *same instrument* measures the same construct across groups (configural/metric/scalar invariance). Path invariance concerns whether *evaluation weight* should depend on the *acquisition pathway* of an attribute once that attribute is itself observed. Different object (evaluator weighting vs. instrument equivalence), different literature.
- Not incremental validity: incremental validity is an empirical ΔR² question about a predictor; path invariance is a normative-cum-conceptual principle about justification for pathway reward. RBV (below) borrows the incremental-validity logic but is framed information-theoretically and normatively.
- Not equality-of-opportunity theory (Roemer 1998): Roemer partitions outcomes into circumstance vs. effort; path invariance is agnostic on the effort/circumstance partition and instead asks what selectors may legitimately reward.
- Not signaling (Spence 1973; Arrow 1973): signaling explains why selectors use proxies; the paper asks what should happen to the proxy's *independent* weight when the target is directly observed — signaling theory does not supply that criterion.
- Not credentialism (Collins 1979): credentialism documents stratifying credential dependence; path invariance supplies a criterion for when that dependence remains justified.
- The English phrase "path invariance" appears sporadically in other literatures (program evaluation, transport) with unrelated meanings; the manuscript defines it precisely and flags the measurement-invariance distinction explicitly.
- **Verdict: GO** — as a named *conditional principle*, not a law.

## Residual Biographical Value (RBV) — MODIFY (retain, demoted)
- Literally the mutual-information generalisation of incremental validity; naming a modest repackaging would overclaim.
- Retained as a *descriptive scaffold* ("residual biographical value", lowercase concept, one informal definition, no claim to a new statistic). Explicitly flagged as conceptually adjacent to incremental validity but broader (non-linear prediction, non-predictive objectives).
- **Verdict: MODIFY** — keep the idea, demote the branding; appears in one section and Figure 3 only.

## Experience–Equality Paradox — MODIFY (retain as descriptive label)
- Substance overlaps strongly with documented dynamics (Snellman et al. 2015 engagement gap; Alvero et al. 2021 essay–income link; Chetty et al. 2023 nonacademic credentials; Espenshade & Chung 2005). What is arguably under-articulated is the *conditional mechanism*: recognising experience can convert opportunity inequality into credential inequality. 
- Retained as a heuristic label for that conditional mechanism, stated without claiming paradox status is novel; framed as synthesising existing findings rather than discovering an effect.
- **Verdict: MODIFY** — keep the label, recast the claim as synthesis.

## Credential Decay — DROP as coined term
- Temporal depreciation of credential informativeness is largely covered by signal-attenuation and human-capital-depreciation ideas plus proximal-evidence logic in personnel selection.
- **Verdict: DROP the term**; keep the intuition inside P5 phrased descriptively ("distal credentials / proximal evidence"), no new name.

## Proxy Capture — DROP as coined term
- Fully covered by Campbell's law, Goodhart's law, Espeland & Sauder's reactivity, and credential-inflation literatures. Coining a near-synonym would be jargon proliferation.
- **Verdict: DROP the term**; the manuscript uses "strategic response to selection weight" / Campbell–Goodhart framing.

## Narrowest defensible novelty claim
A *conditional evaluative principle* for higher-education selection: the legitimate selection weight of biography and experience depends on the information and value they contribute *after* the attributes an institution genuinely values have been observed as directly as practicable — together with the framework (development vs. credential pathway; substitutability; direct-measurement continuum) that operationalises it for admissions.

## Strongest SHE-specific contribution
A principled vocabulary for an unresolved tension in HE scholarship: holistic/experience-sensitive admissions are defended via equity and attacked via privilege, yet the literature lacks a criterion separating experience-as-opportunity-context (contextual interpretation) from experience-as-independent-credential. The paper supplies that criterion.

## Concepts removed to avoid overproduction
- "Proxy capture" (→ Campbell/Goodhart/reactivity).
- "Credential decay" as a named construct (→ descriptive proposition P5).
- No acronym for the paradox mechanism beyond the plain phrase.
