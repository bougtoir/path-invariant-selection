# PROJECT_SCOPE — Path-Invariant Selection

## Central research question
When should the pathway through which competence was acquired matter in higher-education selection? Specifically: when applicants can demonstrate equivalent selection-relevant capabilities through different substitutable pathways, what justification is required for rewarding the pathway itself?

## Target journal (fixed)
Studies in Higher Education (Taylor & Francis). No journal-selection exercise.

## Field architecture
- **Primary case:** university admissions (carries the argumentative burden).
- **Structural generalization:** employment selection (concise).
- **Boundary / limiting case:** political candidate evaluation (≤ ~5–10% of manuscript).

## Core distinction
Experience as **development** (input into learning/competence) vs. experience as **credential** (rewarded independently of demonstrated competence).

## Core model
R (resources) → E (experience) → K (competence) → Y (outcome); selection may add E → Credential → A (evaluation). Path-Invariance Principle: A ⊥ P | Z, conditional on the pathway carrying no independently legitimate, outcome-relevant information.
