## Conceptual-overlap notes (input to novelty audit)
- Measurement invariance (Millsap 2011): same instrument, equal meaning across groups — statistical property of tests. Path invariance concerns *different pathways to an attribute* and *evaluator weighting*, not measurement equivalence.
- Incremental validity (Sackett & Lievens 2008; Schmidt & Hunter 1998): empirical ΔR² question. RBV is the conceptual generalisation (mutual information, not only linear prediction) plus a *normative* claim about justification — overlap is real but not identity.
- Signaling (Spence; Arrow): describes how selectors infer unobservables. Does not ask whether the signal should retain weight once the attribute is directly observed, nor normative criteria for pathway reward. Paper positions itself as asking the *residual* question signaling leaves open.
- Credentialism (Collins; Dore): describes dependence and inflation of credentials. Path invariance supplies an evaluative criterion for when residual credential reliance is justified.
- Contextual admissions (Boliver et al.): context used to interpret attainment relative to opportunity — the paper must protect the distinction between contextual *interpretation* (inside Z) and independent *credential value* of experiences.
