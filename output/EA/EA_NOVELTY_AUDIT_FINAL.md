## Narrowest defensible novelty claim (as used in the manuscript and cover letter)

A pathway-level default warrant for evidence weighting in educational selection, with explicit rebuttal conditions, that distinguishes how much information a biographical source contains (incremental validity) from how much decision weight it is assigned (residual biographical weight). Not claimed: a new validity theory, a new statistic, or a recommendation of any testing technology.
